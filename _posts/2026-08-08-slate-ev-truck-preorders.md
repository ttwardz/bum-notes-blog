[Slate wants to give you an electric pickup truck for $25,000 ][slate][^starting price].

It doesn’t have speakers, or power windows, or a big control screen. It has three climate control buttons. It has a steering wheel that is round.

It has four wheels, two seats, a front trunk, a rear bed, and 205 miles of range.[^estimated]

That sounds like a pickup truck to me.

It looks incredibly boring (like an unfinished toy) in its default gray (which I disagree with as a starting color---even a nicer gray would’ve been an improvement). But it holds so much potential, from vinyl wraps and decals to the interior design and even to exterior hardware components. There are just a bunch of mounting points onto which you can bolt parts.

Gonna quickly call out all the aspirational pickup owners out there, who don’t tow boats or haul lumber or lawnmowers or anything bigger than a cooler and a few chairs to the soccer sidelines. Do your own thing, no hate.

But this makes me aspirational in a different way (because I’m a nerd): I want it because of all the things I can do to it.

- install a custom sound system
- add the rear roll cage to convert it from a pickup into an open-air SUV
- decals, and a wrap
- get somebody to 3D print me extra cupholders, and a phone mount, and a sunglasses holder, and maybe even a purse/bag compartment

I keep thinking of all the things I want in my Jeep that it doesn’t have, and how I could DIY my way to my imagination’s content.

---

## Are people just going to steal all my Slate parts?
The customizability of the Slate truck made me wonder: if I bolt on some custom hardware in my open-air model, or I add parts to the rear bed, or I attach the roll cage, can someone with a screwdriver walk up to my parked Slate truck on the street and unscrew my parts and steal them?

## Is this thing... safe?

---

I remain enamored by, and rooting for, the success of this small, capable, customizable, EV pickup.
