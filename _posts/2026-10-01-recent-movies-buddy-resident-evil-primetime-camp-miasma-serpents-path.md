### Resident Evil (2026)

Anxiety-inducing horror that has no right to be this funny. I had a great time. Dialogue (monologue a lot of the time) = 10/10.

Bit of a chaotic sloppy ending, not sure about all that swinging-around camera work. Lil too much like a bad Marvel fight scene.

The Raccoon City rooftops scene---you know which one I mean---was amazing. The whole icy-road-helping-woman-cop-escort sequence was like a short film in itself.

### Primetime (2026)

Thrilling watch. Also hilarious. Many striking shots. Like, this one’s stunning visually.

R. Pat is captivating. He presents a strange, menacing, yet never quite *intimidating* figure. Truly uncomfortable performance (I mean this as a compliment). Even during the storm scene in the room with his producer, it’s more like he’s awkwardly creepy than dangerous.

Also, Merritt Wever and Matthew Maher are fantastic.

### Buddy (2026)

Bizarre and campy and completely wonderful viewing experience. Laughed and winced throughout. Got caught up in the first ~15 minutes when it’s just the Buddy show, and you’re not zoomed out into the frame of the movie yet.

Absurd and just the right amount of uncomfortable. Ending fell a lil flat---a bit of uncertainty in the world-building aspect that really was unnecessary.

Some great audience reactions in the theater, especially while they’re in the woods with the... two strangers.

Afterward, immediately rewatched [Don’t Hug me I'm Scared](https://youtu.be/9C_HReR_McQ?si=wXec4je6a04UAXaN){:target="_blank"}.

### Teenage Sex and Death at Camp Miasma (2026)

Was hammered after a day at the beach. Was eating chicken tenders at Nitehawk Prospect Park while *that* Gillian Anderson KFC scene was on. (Gillian Anderson is amazing in this movie.)

Was on my own among many dates in the theater. Oof. But I liked the movie. I didn’t know it was the same writer/director (Jane Schoenbrun) as *I Saw the TV Glow* until afterward, and it immediately made so much sense.

A little on the nose with the name “Little Death.” Anyway, the title is very accurate.

*I Saw the TV Glow* was a 10/10 incredible movie. This one was pretty good.

### Serpent’s Path (2024)

Weird, a bit slow but not boring, and pretty funny. Not sure I loved the climax, delivery-wise. Bothered me the way [Christoph Waltz’s evil-Joker-maze](https://youtu.be/gk6FO2iiqCs?si=djQyLXlJu6NoGz6X){:target="_blank"} did in *Spectre*.

Why French? Why remake your own movie, Kiyoshi Kurosawa?

The partner dynamic is a lot of fun. It’s like a buddy cop movie but they’re not cops or buddies, in fact who-even-are-they-and-how-do-they-know-each-other is an important aspect of the plot. Also who-are-all-these-other-people.

Also now I finally get the line from the Barenaked Ladies’ “One Week”:
> Like Kurosawa I make mad films\
> ok I don’t make films\
> but if I did they’d have a samurai
