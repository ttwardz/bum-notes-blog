## Ford announces, not a concept truck, but the concept *of* a truck

[Ford has “announced” the Fathom][ford]---an affordable EV pickup that is an obvious response to the Slate truck.

I hesitate to say “announced”, because what they’ve done is blurted out that, in 2027, they’ll make a $29,000 electric pickup truck. There’s no other information about it, because it’s not real.

Seriously, the webpage has a bunch of pictures on it that are just stock photos of people and dogs and mountains. There’s a person DJing with a turntable, so presumably the truck will feature that equipment, which I’ll admit will be quite the achievement at the price point.

Maybe, Ford has sacrificed one of the seats, or the center console itself, for a DJ rig.


I’m not betting against Ford building a truck.
