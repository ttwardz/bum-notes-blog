[Ford has announced the Fathom][ford]{:target="_blank"}, an affordable EV pickup that seems like an obvious response to [the very cool Slate truck](/2026/08/14/slate-ev-truck-preorders.html).

Or rather, the people at Ford have announced that they are *planning* to make a $29,000 electric pickup truck[^f150] for release in 2027.

There’s no other information about it. It’s nothing more than words on the internet.

Seriously, the webpage features a bunch of stock photos of people and dogs and mountains. There’s a photo of a person riding a bike on the beach. There’s a person DJing with a turntable.

Maybe that’ll be an option---in the vein of the Slate truck’s extreme customizability---to replace the center console with a DJ rig.

Slate couldn’t quite meet its goal for a sub-$20,000 starting price. Ford couldn’t convert F-150 buyers to the F-150 Lightning. We’ll see if either can find the right combination of quality and affordability, or if everyone will just keep buying the Tesla Model Y until humanity stops going outside altogether.


[^f150]: Ford [discontinued its EV F-150 Lightning pickup][wsj]{:target="_blank"} in 2025.
