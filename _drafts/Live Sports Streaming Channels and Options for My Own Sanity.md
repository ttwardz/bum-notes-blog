Live Sports Streaming Channels and Options for My Own Sanity
#bumnotes

*A follow up to last year’s confusion, [I Don’t Know How to Watch Live Sports Anymore](/live-sports/).*

Some updates on the numbers, because the math has changed from last year, and why I didn’t re-subscribe to NFL+ Premium (still a hilarious name) for 2026-2027.

NFL+ Premium is $15/month, or $100/season.

I’m only in it for NFL RedZone, which runs from September to February---6 months. That’s $90 on the monthly plan, so you’ll save money by skipping the annual plan (as long as you remember to cancel after the last playoff game coverage).

This year, I skipped the NFL+ Premium route entirely and added the Sports Extras package to my Sling subscription. It’s an $11/month add-on, so I’m saving money there.

(NFL+ Premium is running a promo for the start of the new season, which brings the annual plan down to $80. But over 6 months, I’ll only pay $66 for the Sling add-on, which I’ll cancel in February, so it’s still a better deal.)

I’ve canceled ESPN+ or Unlimited or whatever that was. There are like four league cup games I want to watch in a year, and they’re better seen at the bar, anyway.
